Lab

Art made after hours: found objects, digital collage, illustration, stop motion and AI experiments.

  • Power: Assemblages of sockets, switches and wires with words, in photographs and short films, where electrical power becomes a metaphor for courage, inspiration and instinct. (2021)
  • AI Works: Personal AI work: a Rajasthan series shot in Midjourney and a red reel, followed by short films, an abstract short and an AI ad. (Ongoing)
  • Eyes: Thirty animated collages about emotions, dreams, fears and the lessons we learn through our eyes. (2021)
  • Birds: A series of animated birds from 2018 to 2020: collages of flowers, leaves and fruit, surreal city-and-nature scenes, and birds drawn in single white lines. (2018–2020)
  • Modern Junkyard: Modern Junkyard is a stop-motion studio: an April Fool film for UrbanClap, dancing vegetables, wool typography and a Holi film for Grofers. (2017–2018)
  • Habitats without the life: AI landscapes built from animal silhouettes with Stable Diffusion and ControlNet. (2023)
  • Absolut Landscapes: Landscapes shaped by the Absolut bottle, made with Stable Diffusion and ControlNet. (2023)
