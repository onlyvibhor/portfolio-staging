Vibhor Mathur: creative director for brand films, campaigns and AI-native production

Vibhor Mathur is a creative director and design leader based in Bengaluru, India. He leads Communication Design at Razorpay across Payments, RazorpayX and POS, and before that spent over six years at Grofers (now Blinkit), leaving as Associate Director, Marketing Design. His work spans brand films, integrated campaigns, interactive landing pages and AI-native production, including Trial of the Century, which won Generative AI at the Shorty Awards. Hire him for creative direction, brand film and campaign work, AI-led production, building an in-house design studio, or design mentoring.

I turn brand briefs into campaigns through design, build tools that scale the visuals, and have fun doing it. Communication Design Senior Manager, Razorpay. Previously Associate Director, Marketing Design at Grofers (now Blinkit).

What I do

  • Creative direction for brand films and campaigns. Concept, art direction and craft for brand films, launch videos, keynote films and integrated campaigns, from brief to final edit.
  • AI-native film and campaign production. Fully AI-made films and campaign systems that stay consistent at scale, such as 81 television films for 20 founders with no cameras, sets or lights.
  • Interactive landing pages and games. Scroll-driven campaign sites and playable games that explain a product by letting people use it.
  • In-house design team leadership. Hiring, developing and leading creative teams, and setting the visual system, process and tools they work in.
  • Custom AI design tools for studios. In-house AI tools with real interfaces for cinematography, product imagery, image editing and founder-led campaigns.
  • Design mentoring and AI-in-design workshops. One-to-one mentoring on ADPList, plus workshops, talks and panels on AI in design and design leadership.
