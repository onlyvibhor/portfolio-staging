Habitats without the life

Landscapes that carry the silhouette of the creatures that belong in them.

AI landscapes built from animal silhouettes with Stable Diffusion and ControlNet.

Year
2023
Tools
Stable Diffusion, ControlNet

Wildlife and habitat are one picture.

A visual representation of the interconnectedness between wildlife and their habitats, and a reminder of the delicate balance of our ecosystems.

Using Stable Diffusion and its ControlNet feature, I made landscapes that blend the essence of iconic creatures with their natural environments.

Start from a silhouette, grow a landscape.

The control images are plain black-and-white silhouettes: a bear, a camel, an elephant and a rhino. Each generated landscape sits next to the shape it was built from, so you can see how the creature's outline organises the terrain.

Tools: Stable Diffusion, ControlNet.
