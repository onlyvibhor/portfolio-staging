Birds

Three years of one subject, in three different languages.

A series of animated birds from 2018 to 2020: collages of flowers, leaves and fruit, surreal city-and-nature scenes, and birds drawn in single white lines.

Year
2018–2020

Birds built from flowers, leaves, fruit and an eye.

The 2020 birds are portraits assembled from petals, leaves, fruit and found fragments, each set on a flat colour, and animated.

Birds in imagined cities and landscapes.

In 2019 the birds moved into surreal, saturated collages: a single bird at the centre of invented cities, gardens and doorways, each in motion.

Birds drawn in single white lines.

The 2018 series reduces each bird to fine white lines on black.
