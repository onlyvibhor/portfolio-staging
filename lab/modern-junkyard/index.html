Modern Junkyard

Our studio of stop motion, with an UrbanClap film at its heart.

Modern Junkyard is a stop-motion studio: an April Fool film for UrbanClap, dancing vegetables, wool typography and a Holi film for Grofers.

Year
2017–2018

Stop motion animation, made one frame at a time.

Modern Junkyard is my stop-motion studio. Its YouTube channel describes it as stop-motion animation for now, with short films, DIY and behind-the-scenes to come. The work so far is small and playful: a paper-craft world for UrbanClap, vegetables that skateboard, juggle and dance, typography written in wool, and short animations for festivals and campaigns.

All Things A: an April Fool digital film for UrbanClap.

The studio's brand film for UrbanClap: a 41-second stop-motion digital film for April Fool's, set in a paper-craft room, with a doll in a saree introduced on screen as Aunty.

UrbanClap All Things A: April Fool digital film

Lettuce Dance

Vegetables with personalities.

The Veggie People series gives everyday produce a life of its own. It includes Lettuce Dance, Citrush Ride, Skateboarding Loki, Juggling with Peace / Peas and Walking Banana.

Grofers Holi Stop Motion Animation

Happy Holi, in colour powder and petals.

A short stop-motion film made for Grofers for Holi, where the greeting is built from colour and petals. It has over 11,000 views on YouTube.

Valentines Week 2018

A week of small animations.

Valentine's week, 2018, told as a grid of Instagram posts, one animation for each day, including Hug Day and Kiss Day.

Wool Typography and festival animations.

The channel also holds a Wool Typography series (Pakoda, Gajar Ka Halwa, Makki ki Roti, Tea and Soup) and an Olitia Diwali stop-motion animation.
