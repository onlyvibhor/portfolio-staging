Make borderless business feel effortless.

Razorpay's Passport For Your Product campaign was a cornerstone of the International Payments launch. Cracking the brief took a new approach: an AI workflow introduced to establish a distinctive campaign design identity, positioning Razorpay as a forerunner in fintech by showing effortless global business expansion.

Where creativity and technology converge.

  1. Imagery. Midjourney conjured the compelling images that formed the campaign's visual backbone.
  2. Enhancement. Photoshop generative fill extended the images to every required dimension without losing their charm.
  3. Animation. The stills were set in motion, turning them into a seamless, flowing narrative.
  4. Edit. The voiceover and visuals were brought together to create a cohesive story.

High relevance, interaction and reach.

The brand campaign beat expectations with high relevance and interaction rates, leading to 35% lower buy rates. It achieved a 50% view-through rate on LinkedIn and substantial engagement on Meta. Distribution across YouTube, LinkedIn and Meta Ads maximised reach, with 94 leads generated, signalling strong interest in Razorpay International Payments.

The process was content too.

The behind-the-scenes video detailing the process was also well received on LinkedIn: over 60,000 impressions, and comments that appreciated the modern approach of using AI tools to push the boundaries of digital marketing.

Stack: Midjourney, Photoshop, Kaiber AI, Adobe Premiere.
