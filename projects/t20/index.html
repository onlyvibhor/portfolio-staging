Put founders on the screen cricket fans are already watching.

The T20 World Cup season is when the nation watches together. Razorpay set out to back its start-up champions where attention already was, treating founders the way the country treats cricketers: with a stadium, a jersey and a player card.

Viewers didn't see an ad. They saw a founder who belonged on that screen.

Designed to look like the broadcast, not interrupt it.

81 films. 20 match dates. Zero shoots. Each founder was lit three ways, warm daylight, floodlit blue and night drama, matched to every match slot.

Every film mirrored cricket player-card aesthetics: centre-composed, sportsman poses, stadium environment.

20 founders. One photo each. No shoots, no studios, no waiting.

A custom avatar tool, built on the Nano Banana API and Gemini AI Studio, took a single reference image and returned a full campaign visual library: sportsman poses, campaign costume, expressions tuned to each founder's personality. 4K output, one click.

Every founder walked into the campaign looking like they had always belonged on that stage.

Every founder. Every platform. One pipeline.

200+ extended social posts carried the campaign forward, including founders who did not appear in the TV spots. Drop in a founder photo and their business story and the pipeline returns a fully composed, campaign-ready post: title layouts per personality, costume, pose, lighting and background, all handled.

No individual design sessions. No back and forth. Just output.

Stack: Gemini, Nano Banana, Replit, ChatGPT, Banner Bear.

The nation reads the paper. We put founders on the front page.

The same pipeline that built the TV assets produced full-page newspaper ads: same founder, same costume, same campaign language, in the hands of the same audience the morning after the match.
