Show how Razorpay lets international businesses accept payments from India.

A dive into the world of international e-commerce payments, focused on Razorpay's solution that lets international businesses accept payments from India and settle in their own currency.

Demystify global e-commerce transactions in under a minute.

The challenge was to demystify the technicalities of global e-commerce transactions. The solution was a vibrant 40 to 60 second animation that makes the intricacies of international payments both understandable and visually compelling.

Complex financial processes, turned into a visually engaging narrative.

Motion graphics and kinetic animation.

The film transforms complex financial processes into a visual narrative using motion graphics and kinetic animation, created with Adobe Illustrator and After Effects. It resonated with the audience, earning over 1000 organic views.

Stack: Adobe Illustrator, After Effects.
