Launch Razorpay payments on WhatsApp.

The campaign for Razorpay's payment solution on WhatsApp, led from concept to completion: coordinating teams, managing the creative process and using AI technology to make a campaign that is both innovative and emotionally resonant.

Shop. Don't abandon.

Melancholy.

Brainstorms with the marketing and design teams produced three campaign concepts. The chosen one, "Melancholy", delved into the emotive narrative of abandoned shopping carts in the digital realm, each with a story echoing the silent plea, "Shop. Don't Abandon."

A solemn start, an upbeat turn.

  1. Imagery. Midjourney created the visual cornerstone: unique, emotionally compelling images of the silent stories of abandoned carts.
  2. Animation. Static images were brought to life into a flowing, dynamic narrative.
  3. Voice. A professional voiceover artist added a solemn tone that turns optimistic and energetic as Razorpay introduces its solution.
  4. Edit. Visuals and voiceover were edited together, with the visual style blending Razorpay blue and WhatsApp green.

The highest engagement rates on Twitter and LinkedIn.

With PR efforts, the launch video amassed over 95K impressions across social channels, with the highest engagement rates on Twitter (10%) and LinkedIn (4.5%). The accompanying blog captivated 6K+ unique readers, and 2000+ businesses expressed interest in the product, including a substantial number of existing Razorpay merchants.

Stack: Midjourney, Kaiber AI, Photoshop, After Effects, Premiere.
