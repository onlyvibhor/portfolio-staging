Break To Build

Thirty founders. Thirty ad films. Zero shoots.

A 100% AI experiment in making a TVC: founders photographed once, then broken, bent and built into cinematic films.

Back the founders building India, on the biggest stage in Indian marketing.

Razorpay's IPL 2026 push on JioHotstar. The ambition was to match the scale and spectacle of the tournament, while also stress-testing everything the design team had learned about AI workflows, tooling and design systems over the year.

A 100% AI experiment in making a TVC. No actors. No stunts. No live-action shots.

What if breaking through was literal?

We took the campaign idea of breaking through and turned it into a visual statement: breaking, bending and defying the impossible.

  • Something Big is Coming | #BreakToBuild
  • 30 Founder Stories of Building Against the Odds | #BreakToBuild

Each one opens on what the founder was told.

Fine jewellery is for special occasions. An honest food brand will never work. Fresh food cannot scale. Pet care is not a need for India. Each film starts with the line a founder heard, and ends with "did it anyway".

  • “Fine jewellery is just for special occasions.” Tamannaah Fine Jewellery did it anyway
  • “An honest food brand? Won’t ever work.” Only What's Needed did it anyway | Revant Himatsingka"
  • They were told "Fresh food can't scale." ID Fresh did it anyway | PC Musthafa
  • "Pet care is not a need for India." Super Tails did it anyway | Vineet Khanna

Getting 30 busy founders in one frame? Almost impossible.

A shoot at this scale means complex schedules and a massive production budget. Breaking glass, breaking walls, breaking ceilings means high-risk shots, high production costs, and founders who are not actors.

AI made it possible. Thirty founders. One frame. Zero logistics.

One shoot and AI. Infinite possibilities.

  1. Shoot. Founders photographed once across carefully planned angles and poses.
  2. Style frames. We designed the visual language and built the worlds each founder would break through.
  3. Generate. AI transformed stills into cinematic environments, action and destruction.
  4. Craft. Shots were refined, composited and edited into finished films.

A stress test for everything the team had learned about AI.

Thirty ad films, a launch film, teasers, music and supporting content, produced AI-led from end to end: not AI-assisted, not AI-enhanced. It took collective conviction to say yes to something with no established playbook, on one of the biggest stages in Indian marketing.
